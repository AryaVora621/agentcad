agentcad is free for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE): personal projects, hobby work, study, research, teaching, and use
by charities, schools, public research bodies and government institutions.

Any other use needs a commercial license. That includes using agentcad inside a company's product, service or internal
engineering work, using it for paid client work, and redistributing it as part of a commercial offering.

## How to get one

Email [arya@arya-vora.org](mailto:arya@arya-vora.org) or reach the author on GitHub
([@AryaVora621](https://github.com/AryaVora621)) with:

- your company name and what you build,
- how you plan to use agentcad (internal design work, in a product, in a hosted service),
- roughly how many people or agents will use it.

You will get a written license with terms and pricing that fit the use. Until you have one, commercial use is not
permitted.

## Why this model

agentcad is source-available so anyone can read, run and improve it, and so individuals, students and researchers
never pay. Businesses that build on it fund its development.
